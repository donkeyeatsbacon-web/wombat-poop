Workshop

Spend Gum Nuts on your catch platform, your wombat and your burrow. Upgrades work in every campaign level, and in endless mode when it's set to Upgraded (Classic stays stock, with its own high scores). In each tree the second tier is a choice of two branches: taking one locks the other, but you can switch later for a fee.

🎨 Your looks

Pure looks, no effect on play. Tap anything to try it on; your wombat wears it in both modes.

Earning Gum Nuts

  • 1 for every poop you stack, and 5 more for a golden one
  • 5 for every finished stack (campaign) or level climbed (endless), 10 more if it never got wobbly
  • 15 for driving off a rival, 10 for wooing a mate

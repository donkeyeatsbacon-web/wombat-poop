COMBAT WOMBAT: POOP STACKER - PLAY-TEST AND RELEASE PLAN
=========================================================
Written 2026-10-13 from a read-through of wombat-poop.html,
wombat-campaign.html, shared/*.js and index.html.


1. WHERE THE GAME STANDS
------------------------

Both modes play from start to finish, and the core is solid: the stacking
physics, the wobble, the lettuce/diarrhea twist, the campaign map and
save/resume all work. tools/compare.js can check that cleanups don't change
behaviour.

What's missing for a public release is mostly not the core game. It's the
things players hit in their first minute: finding both modes, the game running
at the right speed on their screen, sound, being able to pause on a phone, and
understanding the campaign's rules. Section 2 lists the problems found in the
code. Section 3 is how to play-test. Section 4 is the improvement backlog.
Section 5 is the final release checklist.


2. PROBLEMS FOUND IN THE CODE
-----------------------------

Each item says where it is and why it matters. They're ordered by how badly
they hurt a new player.

BLOCKERS (fix before anyone outside plays it)  -- ALL DONE in sprint 1

B1. [DONE] The game runs faster on high-refresh-rate screens.
    Every update is "one step per animation frame" (t++ in update(); fixed
    per-frame gravity, speeds and timers in shared/physics.js and
    shared/wombat.js). On 120 Hz phones (recent iPhones and many Androids)
    and 144 Hz monitors, everything runs 2-2.4x faster, including poops
    falling, the wombat, timers and wobble. The game will feel much harder
    for many players and nobody will know why.
    Fix: run the simulation on a fixed 60 steps/second clock (accumulate
    real elapsed time and run update() 0..n times per frame), and only
    render once per frame. Keep 60 Hz behaviour identical so
    tools/compare.js still passes.

B2. [DONE] There's no way to reach the campaign.
    index.html redirects straight to endless mode, and neither page links
    to the other. The campaign is only reachable by typing its URL.
    Fix: a small title / mode-select screen (or buttons on endless mode's
    title and game-over screens, plus a "back to menu" on the campaign
    intro).

B3. [DONE] Players can't pause on a phone.
    Pause is the P key only (endless), and the campaign has no pause at
    all. On phones there's also no auto-pause: if a call or notification
    pulls the player away, the game picks straight back up mid-fall when
    they return (rAF stops while hidden, so nothing warns them).
    Fix: an on-screen pause button in both modes, and auto-pause on
    'visibilitychange' / 'blur'.

B4. [DONE] Players may get mismatched files after an update.
    The pages load shared/*.js and shared/base.css with plain URLs.
    GitHub Pages caches files for about 10 minutes, so a player can get a
    new page with an old shared script (or the reverse) right after a push,
    and the game can break.
    Fix: add a version query (shared/physics.js?v=7) and bump it whenever
    shared/ changes. A tiny build/deploy step could do this, but by hand is
    fine for now.

IMPORTANT (fix before a wider release)

I1. [DONE] The game has no sound.
    There's no audio of any kind. Plops landing, the strain, a miss, a
    topple, level-up, lettuce crunch and diarrhea are all crying out for
    sound effects, and sound does a lot for the comedy. Needs a mute toggle
    that's remembered, and audio must start only after the first tap
    (browser autoplay rules).

I2. Invading rivals pile up on the burrow (campaign).
    A rival that finishes its march sits exactly on the burrow
    (nodePos() with march = 1 returns the home position). Two or three
    invaders draw on top of each other with overlapping labels, and a tap
    always picks whichever is first in the list. The others can't be
    selected.
    Fix: give each invader its own spot in a ring around the burrow.

I3. Buffs and debuffs are never explained in-game (campaign).
    The overworld shows only their emoji at the bottom. The descriptions
    ("Wider catch platform"...) exist in BUFFS/DEBUFFS but are never
    displayed. The "Trampled" debuff also uses 😤, the same icon as
    rivals.
    Fix: tap an icon to see its name and effect, and pick a different
    icon for Trampled.

I4. [DONE] The on-page hint text sits over the game and assumes a keyboard.
    The #hint line at the bottom of the window draws over the canvas's
    grass on phones, and talks about arrow keys and P to a touch player.
    Fix: show touch or keyboard wording depending on the device, and move
    it into the canvas's own title screen or hide it once play starts.

I5. A campaign loss is abrupt.
    In endless mode the tower falls apart. In a campaign level it instantly
    shows "Failed!" with the tower still standing. The campaign also has no
    way to give up a level and go back to the map.
    Fix: reuse endless mode's topple animation, and add a "retreat" option
    from the pause menu.

I6. [DONE] The font changes from device to device.
    The font stack is Comic Sans MS -> Chalkboard SE -> Comic Neue ->
    cursive, and Comic Neue isn't loaded. Android, Linux and some Windows
    setups fall back to a generic cursive or sans font, which changes the
    look and can push text wider than it was designed for (HUD lines,
    campaign toasts).
    Fix: bundle a free font (e.g. Comic Neue, OFL licence) in shared/.

I7. Emoji look different on every platform.
    Toilet-paper lives, HUD icons, buff icons and 💨 are emoji. They look
    different on each platform, and older Android may show 🧻 as a box.
    Check on real devices (section 3). If they look bad, draw them on the
    canvas the way the poops are drawn.

POLISH (good to have for release)

P1. No favicon, link-preview description or share image. Links shared in
    chats will look bare. Add <meta name="description">, Open Graph tags and
    an icon.
P2. No "add to home screen" support (web app manifest + icons). With an
    offline service worker it would play like an app on phones.
P3. [DONE in sprint 1] Endless mode hides the mouse cursor (cursor: none) on
    the menus and game-over screen too, not just during play.
P4. The campaign has only 4 mate names and 4 rival names, so long seasons
    repeat them a lot.
P5. The endless high score is a single personal best. A short local top-5
    gives players a reason to keep going.
P6. [DONE] Nothing on screen shows how many plops the current level needs, or what
    a new player should do on their first poop. A one-time tutorial prompt
    ("slide here to catch!") would help.


3. PLAY-TEST PLAN
-----------------

Phase A - Check it yourself (after the blockers, about 2 evenings)

  Device matrix. Play 5 minutes of each mode on each:
    [ ] Desktop Chrome or Edge, 60 Hz monitor, mouse
    [ ] Desktop with a 120/144 Hz monitor (or Chrome DevTools frame-rate
        emulation) - checks B1
    [ ] Desktop Firefox
    [ ] Desktop Safari (if a Mac is available)
    [ ] iPhone Safari, portrait and landscape, with notch/home bar
    [ ] Android Chrome, a mid-range or older phone if possible
    [ ] A tablet
    [ ] The game opened straight from disk (file://)

  On each one, check:
    [ ] The text and art are sharp, nothing sits under the notch or home bar
    [ ] It runs smoothly with a tall pile and lots of particles (no stutter)
    [ ] The speed feels the same as on the reference desktop
    [ ] Touch dragging is responsive and the page never scrolls or zooms
    [ ] The high-score name box works with the on-screen keyboard
    [ ] Rotating the device or resizing the window mid-game doesn't break it
    [ ] Leaving and returning (home button, tab switch, phone call) pauses
    [ ] Campaign: progress survives closing the tab mid-season and mid-level
    [ ] Emoji and fonts look right (I6, I7)
    [ ] Sound plays and the mute toggle works (once I1 is done)

  Edge cases to try deliberately:
    [ ] Lose all toilet paper and topple in the same moment
    [ ] Eat lettuce right as a level-up happens
    [ ] Melt the bottom poop of a tall pile (collapse chain)
    [ ] Campaign: let 3+ rivals invade (checks I2), reach 100 charm and
        100 threat on the same day, win and lose seasons, start a new one
    [ ] Private/incognito browsing (storage may be blocked) - should still
        play, just without saving
    [ ] Clear site data mid-campaign and reload

Phase B - Watch other people play (5-8 people, about 1 week)

  Pick testers who have never seen the game: some gamers, some not, at
  least half on phones. Ideally sit with them, or have them screen-record.
  Give no instructions beyond "here's a game".

  Watch for, and write down:
    - How long until they understand they move the platform
    - Whether they find the campaign at all (B2)
    - The first moment they look confused or frustrated
    - What makes them laugh (keep and expand those)
    - Whether they read the Wobble-O-Meter, and if they understand toppling
    - Campaign: do they understand mates vs rivals, charm vs threat, buffs
    - When they stop playing, and why

  Ask afterwards (keep it short):
    1. What was the game about?
    2. What was the most fun moment? The most annoying one?
    3. Did anything feel unfair?
    4. Endless: did it get too hard too fast, or too slowly?
    5. Campaign: did you know what to do on the map?
    6. Would you play again tomorrow? Send it to a friend?

  Optional: keep anonymous play stats in localStorage only (games played,
  average score, level reached, cause of death, campaign win/loss and day).
  Have testers read them off a hidden screen. This is better than guessing
  at balance.

Phase C - Balance pass (after Phase B)

  Tune using the TUNE blocks and the constants they point to. Re-test after
  each change.

  Endless:
    - Time to first topple for a new player (target: 60-90 s first game)
    - The difficulty ramp: poop delay min 70 frames at score >= 133; does
      it plateau too early?
    - Lettuce frequency (0.0016/frame, about one every 10 s of crawling)
      and how punishing the 3.5 s of diarrhea is
    - Does reaching level 2+ feel achievable and rewarding?
  Campaign:
    - Season length (target: 15-25 minutes for a win)
    - Rival speeds/delays vs mates wandering off; threat +5/+10 per rival
      per day can snowball once two invaders arrive
    - Whether the buffs feel strong enough to notice, and the debuffs fair
    - Target stack heights (4-5 rising with waves) vs the campaign's softer
      physics

  Use tools/compare.js whenever you clean up code between tuning changes,
  so cleanups never quietly change the balance.

Phase D - Final check (release candidate, 1-2 days)

  Re-run Phase A's device matrix on the exact build you'll publish, plus
  2-3 fresh testers for a short Phase B.

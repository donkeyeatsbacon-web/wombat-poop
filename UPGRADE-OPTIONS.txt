COMBAT WOMBAT - UPGRADE & PROGRESSION OPTIONS
=============================================
Written 2026-10-13. A menu of branching design options for four ideas:

  1. Rivals who make it into your territory join you on the plank
  2. Mates you woo join you and give perks
  3. An in-game currency
  4. Upgrades you buy for the wombat and the catch platform ("podium")

Each section offers a few directions (A, B, C...) and, where it fits, a
branching tree of upgrades. Pick per section; the last part shows how the
pieces combine and suggests a first slice to build.

Numbers in [brackets] are what the game uses today, so every upgrade below
maps onto something real in the code.


0. WHAT THE GAME ALREADY HAS (so the options build on it)
---------------------------------------------------------

- Rivals on the plank: in a campaign level, every rival that has reached
  your territory already stands on the top plank and rolls lettuce at your
  wombat every 6-10 s. Eating lettuce gives it 3.5 s of diarrhea [210
  frames], which melts poops and shrinks your platform by 30 px a drop
  [up to 90 px, recovering after 30 s].
- Mates: wooing one gives +22 charm and a random buff; she then leaves and
  a newcomer takes her spot. Nobody stays with you.
- Buffs/debuffs (campaign only): wider/narrower platform [+/-40 px on 150],
  slower/faster falling poops [x0.7 / x1.4], +/-1 toilet paper [3], steadier/
  dizzier tower [topple margin 30 px, +22 / -18].
- Things an upgrade can turn: platform width [150 px] and steering
  [9 px/frame on keys, 35% easing to the pointer], toilet paper [3 misses],
  fall speed [gravity 0.16, cap 11 in the campaign], poop sizes [30-78 px],
  poop rhythm [one every ~1.2-2.5 s, faster as score climbs], wobble
  settings [push, jitter, pull upright, damping], topple margin, lettuce
  rate, diarrhea length and damage, score per poop [width / 12].


1. RIVALS WHO JOIN YOU ON THE PLANK
-----------------------------------

The core idea: an invader isn't just a map marker, it's a bad houseguest who
shows up in your levels until you get rid of it. Today they only roll
lettuce. Options to deepen that, roughly in order of how much they change the
game:

  1A. HECKLERS, EXPANDED (smallest change)
      Keep lettuce as their weapon, but give each rival a personality that
      changes how they throw it:
        - Roller   - rolls lettuce along the plank (what exists today)
        - Lobber   - throws it in an arc; you can catch it on the platform
                     to deny it (it lands as a harmless leaf)
        - Rotter   - rotten lettuce: shorter warning, longer diarrhea
        - Bully    - shoves your wombat along the plank, so poops drop
                     somewhere you didn't expect
      Rival personality shows on the map marker, so choosing who to fight
      first becomes a decision.

  1B. SABOTEURS (medium)
      Rivals mess with the tower, not just your wombat:
        - Stomper  - jumps on the plank every so often; the shake adds a
                     wobble kick to your pile
        - Thief    - snatches the top poop of your stack and throws it away
                     (no miss counted, but height lost)
        - Plopper  - poops too: dark "rival cubes" that are heavier or
                     slipperier and score nothing. Catch them and they spoil
                     the stack; dodge them and they don't cost toilet paper.
      This is where "rival poop" lives if you like that joke.

  1C. TURF WAR FOR THE PLANK (biggest)
      Each invader takes up a stretch of the plank, so your wombat has less
      room to crawl and its poops come from a narrower band. With three
      invaders the wombat is penned into the middle. Pairs naturally with
      ways to evict them (below).

  HOW YOU GET RID OF THEM (pick one or more)
    E1. Out-stack them on the map, as today; they go back to their spot.
    E2. Every stack you bank in a level scares one invader off the plank
        for that level (instant, satisfying feedback).
    E3. Catch the lettuce they throw (with 1A's Lobber, or a podium
        upgrade, see 4) and they stomp off in a huff.
    E4. Mates can shoo them (see 2, "Bouncer" perk).

  ESCALATION: as now, each invader adds threat every day; options 1A-1C
  make that threat visible in the levels too, so letting rivals in hurts
  twice.

  RECOMMENDED: 1A + E2. It reuses everything that exists, adds variety
  cheaply, and gives you a way to fight back mid-level. Add 1B's Plopper
  later if you want rival poop.


2. MATES WHO JOIN YOU (perks)
-----------------------------

The core idea: a wooed mate moves into your burrow and comes along to your
levels, sitting on the plank or under the platform, each helping in her own
way. You build a small household over a season.

  2A. HOW MANY CAN COME ALONG?
        a) All of them (simple; power grows all season)
        b) Pick 1-2 before each level (a "team" choice each time)
        c) Room grows: start with 1 seat, earn more seats by upgrading the
           burrow (ties into currency, see 3)
      Recommended: (b), starting with 1 seat and going to 2 at some point.
      Choosing who to bring is a fun small decision and keeps power in check.

  2B. HOW SHE GETS HER PERK
        a) Each mate has a fixed perk tied to her name/personality
        b) When you woo her, you pick 1 of 2-3 perks (a roguelike "draft")
        c) Each mate has a small 2-branch tree; wooing her again later
           (or spending currency) unlocks the next step
      Recommended: (a) to start, (c) once it's working.

  2C. THE PERK CATALOGUE - six personalities, each a 2-branch tree.
      Tier 1 comes with the mate; tier 2 lets you pick one branch.

    GARDENER (deals with lettuce)
      T1 Snack Taker: eats the next lettuce before your wombat does (once per
         level)
        |- T2a Iron Gut: every lettuce gives only half-length diarrhea
        '- T2b Composter: lettuce she eats drops a free poop on your stack

    BUILDER (helps the podium)
      T1 Extra Plank: platform +20 px wider [150 -> 170]
        |- T2a Railings: low rails on the platform ends stop a poop sliding
        |                off once per stack
        '- T2b Quick Fix: a shrunken platform (diarrhea) recovers in 10 s,
                          not 30

    LOOKOUT (helps you aim)
      T1 Spotter: a shadow on the ground shows where the next poop will land
        |- T2a Early Call: you see where the next 2 poops will land
        '- T2b Slow-Mo: the first poop of each stack falls at half speed

    STEADY (helps the tower)
      T1 Steady Paws: tower wobble damps faster [damping .92 -> .89]
        |- T2a Brace: topple margin +15 px
        '- T2b Second Chance: once per level, a tower about to topple
                              straightens up instead (with a big wobble-save
                              animation)

    CHARMER (economy and campaign)
      T1 Sweet Talk: +25% currency from levels
        |- T2a Matchmaker: mates on the map wander off more slowly
        '- T2b Fan Club: +5 charm whenever you win against a rival

    BOUNCER (deals with rivals)
      T1 Glare: invaders throw lettuce 50% less often
        |- T2a Escort: one invader leaves your plank at the start of each
        |              level
        '- T2b Payback: when you bank a stack, an invader loses its nerve
                        and drops threat by 5

  2D. MATE SEAT / PLACEMENT
      Mates could sit (i) on the plank next to your wombat (simple, visible),
      or (ii) on the ground near the catch platform cheering you on (frees
      plank room, good with 1C turf war). Either way they animate and react:
      cheer on a banked stack, gasp at a topple.

  2E. WHAT HAPPENS TO CHARM?
      Today charm wins the season. Options: keep that, and a mate joining is
      a bonus on top; or the season goal becomes "build a household of N
      mates and get threat below X". Recommended: keep charm as is to begin.


3. CURRENCY
-----------

  3A. WHAT IS IT CALLED / WHAT IS IT?
        a) Gum Nuts - Australian bush flavour, drawn as small gumnut icons
        b) Clover - what wombats actually like to eat
        c) Golden Poops - on-brand silly; rare shiny poops worth more
      Recommended: (a) Gum Nuts, with (c) as a bonus: now and then the
      wombat poops a golden cube worth 5 gum nuts if you stack it.

  3B. HOW YOU EARN IT
      Base: 1 per poop stacked. Bonuses, choose any:
        - +5 per stack banked (campaign) / per level climbed (endless)
        - +10 for a stack with no wobble warnings ("clean stack")
        - +15 for beating a rival, +10 for wooing a mate
        - Golden poops (3A c)
        - Daily first-play bonus (light; avoid nagging)
      Target pace: a decent 3-minute run earns ~40-60, so the first upgrade
      (~50) comes after the first good run.

  3C. WHERE YOU SPEND IT
        a) A "Burrow Workshop" screen on the front page (both modes)
        b) Between days on the campaign map only
        c) Both
      Recommended: (a). One shop, one wallet shared by both modes.

  3D. ENDLESS MODE AND FAIRNESS
      Upgrades make high scores easier. Options:
        a) Upgrades apply everywhere; scores just go up (simplest)
        b) Endless has "Classic" (no upgrades, its own top 5) and
           "Upgraded" (separate top 5)
        c) Upgrades are campaign-only; endless earns currency but plays
           stock
      Recommended: (b) if high scores matter to you, otherwise (a).


4. UPGRADE TREES: WOMBAT AND PODIUM
-----------------------------------

Each tree has three tiers. Tier 1 is a straight upgrade. At tiers 2 and 3
you pick one of two branches; the other is locked unless you "respec"
(refund for a fee), so each player's setup ends up a bit different. Costs are
suggestions in gum nuts.

  4A. THE PODIUM (the catch platform)

      T1 Long Board   +15 px                                      50
        |- T2a Wide Load    +25 px more (190 total), but steers 15%
        |                   slower                               120
        |    '- T3a Extendo   ends fold out +20 px for 5 s after
        |                      each banked stack                  250
        '- T2b Nimble Board +15% steering speed, no extra width   120
             '- T3b Dash      double-tap a direction for a quick
                               dodge                              250

    GRIP (what happens when a poop lands)
      T1 Felt Top     poops slide less after landing              50
        |- T2a Sticky Pad   a poop that lands mostly on the board
        |                   never slides off                     120
        |    '- T3a Super Glue  the bottom layer can't topple out  250
        '- T2b Springs      poops bounce lightly and centre
                            themselves                           120
             '- T3b Catapult  a poop landing near the edge hops
                               toward the middle                  250

    STABILITY (the wobble)
      T1 Gyro         topple margin +8 px                         60
        |- T2a Heavy Base  wobble damps faster, tower leans less 140
        |    '- T3a Anchor    once per level, auto-catch a topple
        |                      (like the Steady mate's T2b)       300
        '- T2b Shock Pole  shake from lettuce/diarrhea/stompers
                           doesn't add wobble                    140
             '- T3b Balance Lamp  the wobble meter warns 1 s

    PROTECTION (hazards)
      T1 Awning       diarrhea that hits the board shrinks it 15 px,
                      not 30                                      60

        |                 melting                                140

                          earn 1 gum nut each                    140

  4B. THE WOMBAT (the pooper)
      The player steers the platform, not the wombat, so wombat upgrades
      change the poops it gives you - the "supply side".

    DIET (poop shape and size)                    [30-78 px, random]
      T1 Fibre Diet   poop sizes range 36-70 (fewer tiny and huge)  50
        |- T2a Bricklayer  poops come out squarer and flatter: more
        |                  stable stacks, slightly less score       120
        |    '- T3a Mega Cube  every 10th poop is extra-wide and
        |                       flat: a perfect foundation          250
        '- T2b Big Eater    poops run larger: more score per poop,
                            harder to balance                    120
             '- T3b Golden Belly  golden poops (5 gum nuts) 3x as
                                   often                          250

    AIM (where poops come from)
      T1 Steady Walker  the wombat changes direction less often   50
        |- T2a Centre Line  the wombat drifts toward the middle of the
        |                   plank before it pushes                120
        '- T2b Telegraph    its strain lasts longer, so you get more
                            warning before each poop             120

    TUMMY (lettuce and diarrhea)               [3.5 s diarrhea]
      T1 Tough Tummy  diarrhea lasts 2.5 s                        60
        |- T2a Picky Eater  the wombat ignores 1 lettuce in 3     140
        '- T2b Fertiliser   while it has diarrhea, catching drops
                            on the podium scores points instead of
                            shrinking it                         140

    RHYTHM (how fast poops come)
      T1 Regular       poops come at an even pace (less random)   50
        |- T2a Slow Burn   endless speeds up 30% more slowly      120
        '- T2b Rapid Fire  poops come 15% faster, but each is worth
                           +50% score                           120

  4C. COSMETICS (an optional sink for spare gum nuts)
      Headband colours, a party-hat or crown, platform paint, a trail of
      sparkles on golden poops. Pure looks, no effect. Cheap to make in the
      code-drawn art style.


5. HOW THE PIECES FIT TOGETHER
------------------------------

  Campaign day loop with everything in:
    map -> pick a wombat to visit -> choose which mate(s) come along
    -> level (rivals on the plank harass you; mates help; earn gum nuts)
    -> result -> day passes -> spend gum nuts in the workshop -> map

  Endless loop:
    front page -> workshop (optional) -> endless run (earn gum nuts)
    -> top 5 -> workshop

  Two dials to keep the game from getting too easy:
    - Rivals scale up through the season (more invaders, nastier
      personalities), so your growing household and upgrades are needed.
    - Upgrade prices rise per tier, and branches lock each other out, so no
      one maxes everything.


6. A FIRST SLICE TO BUILD (suggested)
-------------------------------------

  Step 1  Currency + workshop with the PODIUM tree only (4A, tier 1 and 2).
          Proves the economy and the shop screen.        ~1 sprint
  Step 2  Rival personalities on the plank (1A) + "banking a stack scares
          one off" (E2).                                 ~1 sprint
  Step 3  Mates join with fixed perks (2A b, 2B a), 1 seat, 4 of the 6
          personalities.                                 ~1-2 sprints
  Step 4  WOMBAT tree (4B), tier 3s, mate perk trees (2B c), cosmetics.
                                                          ~2 sprints

  Each step is playable on its own, so you can stop or change direction after
  any of them.


7. DECISIONS FOR YOU
--------------------

  1. Rivals: which of 1A / 1B / 1C, and which ways to evict them?
  2. Mates: how many come along (2A), and fixed perks or a draft (2B)?
  3. Currency name and theme (3A)?
  4. Does the shop live on the front page, the campaign map, or both (3C)?
  5. Endless fairness: one top 5, two, or no upgrades in endless (3D)?
  6. Which upgrade trees to start with, and do branches lock each other out
     (or can players buy both eventually)?
  7. Cosmetics: yes or no?

  Any perk or upgrade above can be swapped for something you like better;
  the branch shapes are the point, not the exact list.

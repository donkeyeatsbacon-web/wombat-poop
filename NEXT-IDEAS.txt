COMBAT WOMBAT - IDEAS FOR WHAT TO ADD NEXT
==========================================
Written 2026-10-15, after cosmetics shipped. Everything in UPGRADE-OPTIONS.txt
is now built: rivals on the plank, mates and perks, Gum Nuts and the
Workshop, the platform and wombat trees, harder seasons and cosmetics.

Sizes are rough: S = an afternoon, M = about a sprint, L = two or more.
Each idea notes what it builds on in the existing code.


1. FINISH WHAT'S HALF THERE
---------------------------

  1A. Tier 3 for the wombat's Aim, Tummy and Rhythm trees                 S
      They stop at tier 2 today, while the platform trees go to tier 3.
        Aim:    Centre Line -> Bullseye (every 5th poop drops dead centre)
                Telegraph   -> Shadow (a landing shadow, like the
                                Lookout mate's, for every poop)
        Tummy:  Picky Eater -> Iron Gut (the 2nd lettuce in a row does
                               nothing)
                Fertiliser  -> Compost (each drop on the board gives back
                               a few px of width)
        Rhythm: Slow Burn   -> Second Wind (once per run, the pace resets)
                Rapid Fire  -> Combo (stack 5 without a wobble warning to
                               double Gum Nuts for 10 s)

  1B. Mate perk trees (option 2B c, put off earlier)                      M
      Mates that come along to levels gain experience and grow a stronger
      perk at level 2. For example, a Gardener eats two lettuces, or the
      Digger's pit catches two misses.
      This gives the burrow a reason to matter past season 2, where
      everyone's household is full.

  1C. More rival personalities for late seasons                           S-M
      Season 4+ reuses the full pool. Some ideas:
        Shaker  - stomps the plank so the whole tower sways
        Mimic   - copies the last poop you caught, but rotten
        Gusty   - blows the falling poop sideways
        Sleeper - naps on the plank and blocks the wombat's path
                  until you finish a stack

  1D. A season-end screen that celebrates                                 S
      Today the banner is plain text. Instead, show the mates you won this
      season, Gum Nuts earned, rivals beaten and your best stack, then
      "Season 3 needs 160 charm".


2. NEW WAYS TO PLAY
-------------------

  2A. Puzzle stacks ("Poop Golf")                                         M
      Hand-made levels with a fixed sequence of poops (sizes known in
      advance) and a target shape or height. Score 1-3 stars by misses
      and wobble. The fixed clock already makes runs repeatable, so a
      level is just a list of sizes and a seed.

  2B. Ghost replays                                                       M
      The game is fully deterministic on a seed (it's what compare.js
      relies on). Record your inputs for a best endless run and race a
      faint "ghost" of your own platform next time. This could later be
      shared as a link (see 4B).

  2C. Two-player on one screen                                            L
      Two platforms, arrow keys plus A/D, or two thumbs on a phone. Either
      co-op (one tower, two catchers) or versus (each wombat's lettuce
      lands on the other's side).

  2D. Boss days in the campaign                                           M
      Every 5th day, a "Big Boss" rival (a huge wombat) sits on the plank
      for a whole level: several personalities at once. Beat them for a
      cosmetic only bosses drop.

  2E. A short story mode / tutorial island                                M
      Three or four guided levels that teach steering, wobble, lettuce and
      rivals one at a time, before the open campaign map.


3. MORE VARIETY IN A RUN
------------------------

  3A. Habitats per season                                                 M
      Season 1 is grassland, then desert (heat makes poops slide more),
      snow (icy board, less grip), night (darker sky, glowing golden
      poops). This is mostly art plus one tuning knob per habitat in TUNE.

  3B. Weather that changes mid-level                                      S-M
      Wind gusts push falling poops sideways, rain makes the board
      slippery for 10 s, and a rainbow doubles golden poops briefly.
      Shown with a forecast icon on the map.

  3C. New special poops                                                   S each
        Bouncy poop - springs once on landing
        Heavy poop  - small but steadies the tower
        Ice cube    - slides unless it lands dead flat
        Mystery box - random buff when stacked
      These would hang off the same hue trick as golden and bad cubes.

  3D. Power-ups you catch                                                 S
      Occasional items falling with the poops: a glue pot (next 3 poops
      stick), a magnet (pulls the next poop to the middle), a clock
      (slow-mo for 5 s).


4. GOALS AND SHOWING OFF
------------------------

  4A. Achievements / a sticker book            BUILT 2026-10-15 (stickers)  M
      Some examples:
        - "Stack 20 without a wobble warning"
        - "Win a season with no mates along"
        - "Catch a Lobber's lettuce 10 times"
        - "Own every hat"
      Each gives a sticker in a book on the front page, and some unlock
      cosmetics you can't buy. This adds goals without a daily bonus.
      (You turned the daily bonus down, so nothing here nags.)

  4B. Share card                                                          S
      On game over, a "Share" button builds an image (your wombat in its
      outfit, the score, the tower) using the same art code as the icons,
      then opens the phone's share sheet.

  4C. Stats page                                                          S
      Totals across all play: poops stacked, Gum Nuts earned, seasons won,
      favourite mate, rival most often beaten, tallest tower ever.

  4D. Online leaderboard                                                  L
      Needs a small server (or a hosted service), so it's the biggest
      jump from "static site" to running something. The Classic list
      would be the fair one to put online.


5. FEEL AND POLISH
------------------

  5A. Music                                                               M
      A light looping bush-band tune, synthesised like the sound effects
      (no files), that picks up tempo as the tower grows. A separate mute
      for music and effects.

  5B. Phone vibration on catches, topples and golden poops                S
      navigator.vibrate where supported, with a setting to turn it off.

  5C. Juice                                                               S
      Squash-and-stretch on the platform when a big poop lands, a slow-mo
      zoom on the toppling poop, and confetti when a season is won.

  5D. More cosmetics                                                      S each
      More hats (pirate, chef, viking), glasses, capes, a platform trail
      and paint for the top plank. Some unlocked only by achievements or
      bosses, so they mean something.

  5E. Settings screen                                                     S
      Sound and music volume, vibration, reduced motion (less shake and
      wobble animation) and a bigger-text option.


6. REACH AND ACCESSIBILITY
--------------------------

  6A. Reduced motion and colour-blind safe cues                          S
      Golden vs bad cubes and the wobble meter rely on colour. Add shapes
      or patterns.

  6B. Left-handed / one-thumb controls on phones                         S
      Drag anywhere to steer already works. Check that the pause button
      and toasts are reachable and readable on small screens.

  6C. Translations                                                        M
      Pull the on-screen text into one table so it can be translated.


7. UNDER THE HOOD
-----------------

  7A. Turn the scratch test harnesses into tools/                         S
      The season, mate, rival and outfit runs I used for testing live
      outside the repo. Folding their save-seeding (SAVE / PROG) into
      tools/compare.js would make every future change easier to check.

  7B. Stop the auto-saver catching temporary files                        S
      It committed temporary edits to .claude/launch.json three times this
      week. Have it skip that file, or move preview config outside it.

  7C. Split wombat-campaign.html                                          M
      It's ~1,300 lines with the map, levels, rivals, mates and picker all
      in one script. Moving rivals and mates into shared/ files would make
      the next big feature easier.

9. IF I HAD TO PICK THREE
-------------------------

  1. Playtest + balance pass (section 8). The game has grown a lot since
     the last time someone played it end to end.
  2. Achievements / sticker book (4A), which also feeds cosmetics (5D).
     It gives long-term goals without nagging.
  3. Habitats per season (3A). Each new season currently looks the same,
     and a new place each season makes winning one feel like progress.
